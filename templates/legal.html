Your privacy matters

Shield Pharmacy collects only the information needed to provide accounts, fulfil orders, process payments, respond to messages and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Information we use

Account and contact details, delivery information, order history, payment status, consent preferences and essential security logs. Payment credentials are processed by approved providers and are not stored by Shield.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion where Kenyan law permits. Contact care@shieldpharmacy.co.ke for privacy requests.

Security and retention

We apply access control, encryption in transit, secure password hashing and limited retention. Information is retained only for service, regulatory, fraud prevention and accounting needs.

Using Shield Pharmacy

By ordering, you confirm that the information supplied is accurate and that you are legally able to purchase the selected products. Product information supports informed shopping but does not replace diagnosis or professional medical advice.

Orders and delivery

Orders are subject to stock verification and pharmacist review where required. Delivery estimates may change because of location, weather or verification needs. We will communicate material changes.

Payments and returns

Available payment methods appear at checkout. Unopened, eligible goods may be returned according to Kenyan consumer law; medicines and hygiene-sensitive items may be restricted for safety.

Responsible purchasing

Prescription-only medicines are not sold through the current storefront. The future prescription workflow will require valid documentation and pharmacist approval.
